Risk-Driven Management (RDM) Framework Master Document

The Risk-Driven Management (RDM) Framework Master Document is the canonical reference for the RDM framework. It defines the authoritative concepts, terminology, governance model, operating rules and processes of RDM, including Commitment, Threat, Risk, Issue, Warning, TIP, TAP and RCP.

Current Edition

Version 3.00 – Canonical Reference Edition
Document Reference: RDM-FMD-3.00
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20810447

Version 3.00 introduces the formal treatment of Internal Policy as the source of Internal Constraints, clarifies the governance and routing of Warnings, strengthens the relationship between Delivery Contracts and Internal Policies, and adds the Commitment Definition Process (RDM-CDP).
