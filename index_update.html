Risk-Driven Management (RDM)

Risk-Driven Management (RDM) is a management framework founded on the principle that management exists to make and fulfil Commitments under conditions of uncertainty. It places Risk evidence at the centre of management control and provides a common control model that scales from strategic leadership through programme, project, operational and work-package delivery.

Canonical Reference

The authoritative specification of the framework is the Risk-Driven Management (RDM) Framework Master Document, Version 3.00.

  • Document Reference: RDM-FMD-3.00
  • Status: Canonical Reference Edition
  • DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20810447

Version 3.00 formalises the role of Internal Policy as the source of Internal Constraints, clarifies the routing of Warnings to Policy owners, strengthens the symmetry between Delivery Contracts and Internal Policies, and introduces the Commitment Definition Process (RDM-CDP).

The Framework Master Document is the primary reference for all future RDM publications, training material, derivative guides and software implementations.
