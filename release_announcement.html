Risk-Driven Management Framework Version 3.00 Published

23 June 2026

Version 3.00 of the Risk-Driven Management (RDM) Framework Master Document has now been published as the current Canonical Reference Edition of the framework.

This release incorporates substantive framework refinements identified during the design of the RDM App and strengthens the framework’s treatment of internal governance. In particular, Version 3.00:

  • establishes Internal Policy as the source of Internal Constraints;
  • formalises the tagging of Internal Constraints to named Policies and Policy owners;
  • clarifies the routing of Warnings through the policy ownership chain;
  • strengthens the symmetry between Delivery Contracts and Internal Policies;
  • introduces the Commitment Definition Process (RDM-CDP).
