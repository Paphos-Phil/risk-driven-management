Version History

Version 3.00 — Canonical Reference Edition

Publication date: 23 June 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20810447

Version 3.00 is the current canonical edition of the Risk-Driven Management Framework Master Document. It formalises Internal Policy as the source of Internal Constraints, introduces mandatory policy tagging of constraints, clarifies the routing of Warnings to Policy owners, strengthens the symmetry between Delivery Contracts and Internal Policies, and adds the Commitment Definition Process (RDM-CDP).

Version 2.00 — Canonical Reference Edition

Version 2.00 established the first canonical public edition of the framework and consolidated the core definitions of Commitment, Threat, Risk, Issue, Delivery Contract, Managed Workspace and the TIP–TAP–RCP control model.

Version 1.00 — Foundational Publication

Version 1.00 recorded the initial public statement of the framework and established the first canonical publication baseline.
